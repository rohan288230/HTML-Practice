Artificial Intelligence Is Changing Modern Software Development

By Rohan Singh Ramola

Published on September 1, 2026

Artificial intelligence is becoming an important part of modern software development.

AI-Assisted Development

Developers are using AI tools to generate code, analyze errors and improve productivity.

However, developers still need strong programming fundamentals to understand and verify generated code.

Good developers use AI as a tool, not as a replacement for understandiing.

The Future pf Development

Software development is likely to become increasingly collaborative between humans and intelligent tools.

New Web Technologies Continue to Evolve

Modern web developers are learning new frameworks, tools and development practices.
